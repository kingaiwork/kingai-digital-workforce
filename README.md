# KINGAI AI Digital Workforce & Smart Merchant System

**[中文版](#中文版)** · Website: [kingai.work](https://kingai.work) · Contact: vip@kingai.work

An AI digital workforce for small and mid-sized merchants and brand teams: your website stays managed, inquiries get answered, and content keeps flowing — so daily online operations no longer depend on someone watching the screen all day.

## The problem

Running a business online is a pile of small jobs that all need a person:

- **Slow replies.** Inquiries arrive at night and on weekends. If they sit until the next morning, the customer has already gone elsewhere.
- **Websites go stale.** Building the site is only the start. Updates, optimization and health checks need ongoing attention that most small teams simply don't have.
- **Too many accounts.** Every new channel means another sign-up for customers, and another back office for the merchant to switch between.

## What KINGAI does

- **Smart websites** — more than a brochure page. Inquiry intake sits up front, common questions are answered by AI first, and each request is sorted by urgency before it reaches the merchant.
- **AI customer service and inquiry triage** — questions are classified as they come in. The straightforward ones get answered on the spot; the ones that need a person are routed out, instead of lying in an inbox overnight.
- **Content and operations automation** — publishing, search and AI-search optimization, and site health checks run on a fixed rhythm. People only handle the exceptions the system flags.
- **One account everywhere** — merchants and customers use a single account across every site in the network. One ledger, no re-registering at each stop.

## Running in production, not on slides

This system already runs our own business, and every part of it can be checked in public:

- A community website network spanning **nine country sites**, with publishing, optimization and health checks run by the system around the clock.
- A **unified account system** live across that whole network.
- **KingAI Cloud**, a cloud services platform built on the same foundation.

We use it ourselves first. Whether it works is not a matter of presentation — the sites above are live.

This repository is a public showcase for the project. The system itself is proprietary: no source code, implementation details, or internal documentation are published here.

# KINGAI AI 数字员工与智慧商家系统

**[English](#kingai-ai-digital-workforce--smart-merchant-system)** · 官网：[kingai.work](https://kingai.work) · 联系：vip@kingai.work

给中小商家和品牌团队配一名能干活的 AI 数字员工：网站有人管，询盘有人接，内容有人写，日常运营不再全靠人盯。

## 问题在哪

线上经营的事情不大，但件件要人：

- **响应慢。** 顾客的询盘不挑时间，晚上和周末照样来。等第二天再回，人已经找了别家。
- **网站建完就荒。** 建站只是开始，后面还有更新、优化、检查。多数小商家没有专职的人管这些，站点慢慢就没人管了。
- **账号各管一摊。** 每开一个新渠道，顾客就多一套账号密码；商家自己也要在几个后台之间来回切换。摩擦攒多了，两边都累。

## 系统做什么

- **智慧网站** —— 不只是展示页。询盘入口前置，常见问题由 AI 先答，顾客留下的需求按轻重缓急分好类，再交到商家手里。
- **智能客服与询盘分诊** —— 问题进来先判断类型：能直接答的答掉，需要人跟进的转出来，不让询盘躺在收件箱里过夜。
- **内容与运营自动化** —— 内容发布、面向搜索引擎与 AI 搜索的优化、站点状态巡检，按固定节奏自动执行。人只处理系统报上来的异常。
- **统一账号（一号通）** —— 商家和顾客共用一套账号走全部站点，只有一本账，不用走到哪注册到哪。

## 已经在跑，不是演示稿

这套系统已经在我们自己的业务里运行，每一项都能公开核对：

- 覆盖**九个国家站点**的网站群，新闻更新、搜索优化与站点巡检由系统全天候自动运行。
- 已上线并扩展到整个站群的**统一账号系统**。
- 建在同一套基础之上的云服务平台 **KingAI Cloud**。
